## Limitations

- **Customer subscriptions only.** Supplier (purchase) subscriptions are never anchored, consolidated or credited, and raise no Payment Requests.
- **Month and year plans only, for anchors.** Day and Week plans, plans priced by **Monthly Rate**, and, when the customer is billed yearly, monthly plans whose **Billing Interval Count** does not divide 12 cannot be anchored. **Follow Calendar Months** cannot be combined with an anchor. See [What cannot be anchored](anchored-billing.md#what-cannot-be-anchored).
- **No Pricing Rules on anchored invoices.** The invoices the engine makes for an anchored customer ignore Pricing Rules, so customer-specific pricing has to be on the plan.
- **One gateway per invoice.** A consolidated invoice whose plans point to different payment gateways is not charged automatically.
- **Backdated start dates are limited.** For anchored and consolidating customers, start dates too far in the past are refused; load such subscriptions as an import and replay the periods. See [Backdated start dates](anchored-billing.md#backdated-start-dates).
- **One period per run.** A consolidating customer's subscription that is more than one period behind is billed one period per daily run.
- **No credit for postpaid subscriptions.** A subscription billed at the end of the period gets ERPNext's invoice for the days used when it is cancelled, not a Credit Note.
- **No gateway refunds.** A Credit Note leaves the credit with the customer. Refunding it through the gateway is up to you.
- **No settlement from GoCardless.** With the stock Frappe Payments app, GoCardless charges the mandate but does not mark the invoice paid: its webhooks handle mandate events only. Make the Payment Entry for a collected payment by hand, or use a Payments build that handles payment events.
- **Duplicate lines.** Two lines with the same item and description cannot share a consolidated invoice unless Selling Settings allows an item more than once. Give each subscription its own **Service Identifier**.
- **No app-wide payment delay.** When a payment is collected comes from **Days Until Due** on each Subscription. Earlier versions had an **Auto-billing Delay** setting that nothing read; `bench migrate` removes it.

## Troubleshooting

Messages in a dialog stop the save or action that raised them. Messages from the daily run, a Process Subscription or a background job appear in the **Error Log** instead: the daily run writes them under ERPNext's title "Subscription failed", or under the titles below.

### Payment Requests

| Message or Error Log title                                                                                                                                                       | Cause                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Fix                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No Payment Request after an invoice is submitted                                                                                                                                 | No plan on the invoice has a **Payment Gateway**, the invoice is a return or has nothing outstanding, or the kill switch is set.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Set **Payment Gateway** on the plan, or remove `subscription_auto_charge_disabled` from the site configuration. Raise the request for this invoice by hand. |
| "Mixed payment gateways on subscription invoice `<INVOICE>`"                                                                                                                     | The plans on the invoice name two or more different gateways.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Raise the Payment Request by hand, and point the customer's plans to one gateway.                                                                           |
| "Stale subscription invoice not auto-charged: `<INVOICE>`", and the comment "Left as draft: due date `<DUE_DATE>` is more than `<DAYS>` days in the past. Charge manually."      | The invoice's due date is older than **Auto-charge Max Lateness (days)**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Check the draft Payment Request and submit it to charge, or delete it.                                                                                      |
| The customer got a payment link e-mail instead of being charged                                                                                                                  | The gateway did not take the payment itself: for GoCardless, the customer has no active **GoCardless Mandate**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Set up the mandate. Later invoices are charged without an e-mail.                                                                                           |
| "Subscription payment not confirmed: `<PAYMENT_REQUEST>`", and the comment "Payment not confirmed: the payment gateway neither confirmed a payment nor returned a payment link." | The gateway's check raised an error (a gateway that cannot be reached, for example), or GoCardless's payments API failed or refused the payment. ERPNext's `payment_gateway_validation` gives the same answer for these as for a charge, and no Integration Request against the request is Authorized or Completed. The request stays Requested, unpaid and un-e-mailed. | Check the gateway's settings, the Integration Request and the Error Log, confirm in the gateway that nothing was charged, then collect the payment by hand. |

### Customer settings

| Message                                                                               | Cause                                                                      | Fix                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Subscription Billing Anchor Date is required for Anniversary anchoring."             | **Subscription Billing Anchor** is Anniversary with no date.               | Set **Subscription Billing Anchor Date**, or choose Calendar. |
| "Customer `<CUSTOMER>`: Anniversary anchoring needs subscription_billing_anchor_date" | The anchor date was removed outside the form, for example by a data patch. | Set the date on the Customer and save.                        |

### Anchored billing

| Message                                                                                                                                                                                     | Cause                                                                                                                                                                                      | Fix                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Follow Calendar Months cannot be combined with a customer billing anchor; clear it."                                                                                                       | **Follow Calendar Months** is ticked on a subscription of an anchored customer.                                                                                                            | Untick it: the anchor already decides the periods.                                                                                                                              |
| "Plan `<PLAN>` uses Monthly Rate pricing, which cannot be prorated or anchored."                                                                                                            | The plan's **Subscription Price Based On** is Monthly Rate.                                                                                                                                | Use a plan priced by **Fixed Rate** or **Based On Price List**.                                                                                                                 |
| "Subscription `<SUBSCRIPTION>`: cannot anchor a Day plan" (or Week)                                                                                                                         | The plan's **Billing Interval** is Day or Week.                                                                                                                                            | Use a Month or Year plan, or clear the customer's anchor.                                                                                                                       |
| "Subscription `<SUBSCRIPTION>`: a 5-month plan does not divide a year"                                                                                                                      | The customer is billed yearly and the plan's **Billing Interval Count** does not divide 12.                                                                                                | Use a plan of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 12 months, or set **Subscription Billing Interval** to Month.                                                                                    |
| "Plan `<PLAN>` resolves to a zero rate (missing Item Price?)"                                                                                                                               | The plan's rate came out as zero, usually because its price list has no Item Price for the item.                                                                                           | Add the Item Price, or give the plan a **Cost**.                                                                                                                                |
| "Start Date `<DATE>` lies in a billing period that has already ended, so the period starting `<DATE>` would only be billed once the first invoice is paid and is dropped when it ends. ..." | The start date is in a period that has ended, and **Generate New Invoices Past Due Date** is off.                                                                                          | Tick **Generate New Invoices Past Due Date**, or load the subscription as an import: see [Load subscriptions with a history](run-billing.md#load-subscriptions-with-a-history). |
| "Start Date `<DATE>` is too far in the past for the elapsed periods to be billed (they reach one billing cycle past the first period end). ..."                                             | The first invoice is left to the daily run (a consolidating customer, or **Mid-term Billing Mode** on Next Daily Run) and today is a full period or more past the end of the first period. | Load the subscription as an import and replay the periods. The message also suggests **Generate New Invoices Past Due Date**, but this check applies with it ticked too.        |

### Consolidated invoices

| Message or Error Log title                                                                                                                                                          | Cause                                                                                                                                                  | Fix                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Consolidated billing failed: `<CUSTOMER>`" (Error Log)                                                                                                                             | Building or submitting the customer's invoice failed. The log holds the traceback, and nothing of that group was kept.                                 | Fix the cause in the log. The next run bills the group.                                              |
| "Consolidated billing for `<CUSTOMER>` failed: `<ERROR>`"                                                                                                                           | The `errors` of a group in the summaries `run_consolidated_billing` returns: building or submitting the invoice failed, and the group was rolled back. | Fix the cause, which is also in the Error Log, and run again.                                        |
| "Consolidated billing scan failed: `<SUBSCRIPTION>`" (Error Log)                                                                                                                    | The subscription could not even be checked for being due, usually because of a plan that cannot be anchored.                                           | Fix the plan or the subscription. The customer's other subscriptions are billed without it.          |
| "Subscription `<SUBSCRIPTION>`: identical item and description already on `<INVOICE>`; set a Service Identifier or enable Selling Settings > Allow Item to Be Added Multiple Times" | Two subscriptions of the same item have the same description. The setting is labelled **Allow same Item to be added multiple times in a transaction**. | Give each subscription its own **Service Identifier**, or tick that setting in **Selling Settings**. |
| "Subscription `<SUBSCRIPTION>` produced a zero-rate line; core would re-price it at list price."                                                                                    | A line came out with a zero rate or quantity.                                                                                                          | Check the plan's price and the subscription's quantity.                                              |
| "Consolidated billing for `<CUSTOMER>` failed earlier in this run; not billing `<SUBSCRIPTION>` standalone"                                                                         | One of the customer's groups failed in this run, so the others are not billed one by one.                                                              | Fix the cause of the first failure in the Error Log.                                                 |
| "Billing for `<CUSTOMER>` is running elsewhere"                                                                                                                                     | Another run, or a cancellation, held the customer's billing lock for more than 60 seconds.                                                             | Wait for the other run to finish and run again.                                                      |
| "Consolidated billing for `<CUSTOMER>` hit a deadlock"                                                                                                                              | The database aborted the group because of a lock conflict with another transaction.                                                                    | Run again.                                                                                           |
| "Process Subscription (customer) failed" (Error Log)                                                                                                                                | An unexpected error stopped a Process Subscription that had a **Customer** set.                                                                        | Fix the cause in the log and submit a new Process Subscription.                                      |
| "Subscription status refresh failed: `<SUBSCRIPTION>`" (Error Log)                                                                                                                  | Refreshing one subscription's status after an invoice was cancelled or paid failed. The other subscriptions were refreshed.                            | Open the subscription and use **Actions** > **Fetch Subscription Updates**.                          |

### Cancellation with a credit note

| Message                                                                                       | Cause                                                                                    | Fix                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Credit note `<CREDIT_NOTE>` already credits row `<ROW>` of `<INVOICE>`."                     | A submitted return already credits that line.                                            | Check the existing Credit Note. To cancel without a new credit, untick **Credit Note On Cancellation** for the moment. |
| "Row `<ROW>` of `<INVOICE>` has already been fully returned."                                 | The whole quantity of that line has been returned already.                               | As above.                                                                                                              |
| "`<INVOICE>` carries an Actual-amount tax row; issue this credit note manually."              | The source invoice has a tax row of type Actual, which cannot be scaled.                 | Make the Credit Note by hand.                                                                                          |
| "Row `<ROW>` of `<INVOICE>` carries no service period; issue that credit note manually."      | An old invoice line has no period, on the line or in the invoice's **From Date**.        | Make the Credit Note by hand.                                                                                          |
| "`<SUBSCRIPTION>` is billed in arrears; nothing is prepaid. Use Cancel Subscription instead." | `cancel_with_credit_note` was called for a subscription billed at the end of the period. | Use **Actions** > **Cancel Subscription**.                                                                             |
| "Credit Note On Cancellation is disabled in Subscription Settings."                           | `cancel_with_credit_note` was called with the setting off.                               | Tick the setting, or use **Cancel Subscription**.                                                                      |
| "Credit notes on cancellation are only supported for Customer subscriptions."                 | `cancel_with_credit_note` was called for a supplier subscription.                        | Use **Cancel Subscription**.                                                                                           |
| "subscription is already cancelled."                                                          | The subscription is already Cancelled.                                                   | Nothing to do.                                                                                                         |
